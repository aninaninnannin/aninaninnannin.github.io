About

I’m Graham Lyu, a media artist from Beijing, working between New York and China. I’m drawn to stories and the emotions within human relationships. I also enjoy playing with technology, exploring what it can do and using it to bring my ideas into the world.

Making is a form of self-expression for me. In Talk to her, I created a space for a conversation I could no longer have: speaking with my grandmother in Korean. Other projects begin with the curiosity to try something, build something, and see where it goes.

People bring their own experiences to my work. I don’t look for a single interpretation, but I value the moments when something personal to me resonates with someone else.
